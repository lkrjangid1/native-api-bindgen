Legal and source policy

This page describes engineering policy. It is not legal advice; organizations should do their own review before redistributing generated bindings.

What the tool does

  • Reads SDKs installed on your machine (Android SDK, Xcode) in place, using their official files and tools.
  • Generates declarations, binding code, symbol names, availability metadata and links to official documentation — never copied documentation text.
  • Keeps generated bindings local by default (generatedArtifacts: local-only).

What it never does

  • Download, vendor, commit or publish SDK files (jars, headers, frameworks, binaries).
  • Expose hidden, non-SDK or private APIs as supported.
  • Keep Clang AST dumps or Swift symbol graphs outside temporary directories.

native-api-bindgen audit-license checks a project for SDK artifacts, copied headers and documentation, foreign licenses and generated SDK-derived files, and fails on blocking findings.

Open items

Redistribution of generated Android or Apple bindings (for example publishing them as packages) has not been reviewed by counsel and is not enabled by default.
