# Governance

- **Maintainers** review and merge pull requests, cut releases and own the roadmap. The initial maintainer is the repository owner.
- **Decisions** are made by consensus on issues/PRs. Changes to the IR schema, the type-mapping contract, the ownership model, or the legal/source policy require an issue describing the change and approval from a maintainer.
- **Legal/source policy changes** are never merged on the basis of assumption; uncertain items stay marked `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
- **Becoming a maintainer:** sustained, high-quality contributions and reviews; nominated by an existing maintainer.
